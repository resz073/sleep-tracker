Privacy policy

Sleep Tracker for Android. Effective 29 October 2026.

The short version

Sleep Tracker keeps your sleep log in your own account so it syncs between your devices. It has no ads and no analytics, and your data isn’t sold or shared with anyone for their own use.

What the app collects

  • Account details. Your email address. If you sign in with Google, Google also shares your name and profile picture with the sign-in service.
  • Sleep entries you add. When you went to bed and woke up, your quality ratings, and any sleep that’s in progress.
  • Birth year, if you add it. Used only to show the amount of sleep recommended for your age and keep your sleep goal within that range.
  • Goal and schedule settings. Your sleep goal and when you changed it, your wake-up time if you set one, how long you take to fall asleep, and your weekend days.

Reminder settings stay on your phone. Reminders are scheduled on your phone, not sent from a server.

The app doesn’t collect your location, contacts, photos or any data from other apps.

How it’s used

Only to run the app: signing you in, saving and syncing your entries, suggesting a sleep goal for your age, and working out your statistics and bedtime schedule.

Where it’s stored

Your account and entries are stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which Sleep Tracker uses as its service provider. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access rules let only you read or change your own entries. To keep the service secure, Firebase also processes technical information such as IP addresses.

A copy of your entries is also kept on your phone so the app works offline.

Sharing

Your data isn’t sold, rented or shared with third parties for their own purposes. Google processes it on Sleep Tracker’s behalf to provide the service.

How long it’s kept

Your data is kept until you delete it. Deleting an entry removes it. Deleting your account removes your account and all your entries straight away. Copies may remain in Google’s backups for a limited time before they’re overwritten.

Your choices

  • Export: Settings → Backup → Export CSV saves all your entries as a file.
  • Delete: Settings → Account → Delete account removes your account and data. You can also ask for deletion by email.
  • Reminders: turn them on or off in Settings, or block notifications in Android Settings.

Children

Sleep Tracker is for people aged 13 and over. It isn’t directed at children under 13 and doesn’t knowingly collect data from them. The birth year you can choose in the app starts at age 13.

Health information

Sleep Tracker is not a medical device and doesn’t give medical advice. Talk to a doctor about any sleep problem.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
