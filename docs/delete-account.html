Delete your Sleep Tracker account

How to delete your Sleep Tracker account and all the sleep data linked to it.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Sleep Tracker and tap the settings icon at the top right.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with your password, or with Google if you signed in with Google.

Your account and all your entries are deleted straight away.

By email

If you can’t use the app, email reshad.rahman@yahoo.com from the email address you use with Sleep Tracker, with the subject Delete my Sleep Tracker account. Your account and data will be deleted within 30 days, and you’ll get a reply when it’s done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: your email address and, for Google sign-in, the link to your Google account.
  • All your sleep entries and ratings, your birth year, your sleep goal and your schedule settings.

Nothing is kept after deletion. Copies may remain in Google’s backups for a limited time before they’re overwritten.

Delete some of your data, and keep your account

You can delete any sleep entry without deleting your account:

  1. Open Sleep Tracker and tap History at the bottom.
  2. Find the entry. Use the arrows to move between weeks.
  3. Tap × next to the entry. It’s deleted from your account and all your devices straight away.

Only that entry is deleted. Your account and your other entries are kept. You can also email reshad.rahman@yahoo.com from your account’s email address to ask for specific entries to be deleted. That’s done within 30 days.

Want to keep your data first? In the app, use Settings → Backup → Export CSV before deleting.
