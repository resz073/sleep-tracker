Delete your Sleep Tracker account

How to delete your Sleep Tracker account and all the sleep data linked to it.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Sleep Tracker and tap the settings icon at the top right.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with your password, or with Google if you signed in with Google.

Your account and all your entries are deleted straight away.

By email

If you can’t use the app, email reshad.rt0@gmail.com from the email address you use with Sleep Tracker, with the subject Delete my Sleep Tracker account. Your account and data will be deleted within 30 days, and you’ll get a reply when it’s done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: your email address and, for Google sign-in, the link to your Google account.
  • All your sleep entries, ratings and schedule settings.

Nothing is kept after deletion. Copies may remain in Google’s backups for a limited time before they’re overwritten.

Want to keep your data first? In the app, use Settings → Backup → Export CSV before deleting.
