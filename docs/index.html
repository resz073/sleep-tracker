Sleep Tracker

Log your sleep in two taps, see your sleep debt, and get a bedtime that fits your wake-up time.

What it does

  • Tap Going to sleep at night and I’m awake in the morning. Your night is logged.
  • See your sleep against an 8-hour goal, by day, week and month, with a 14-day sleep debt.
  • Get a bedtime worked out from your usual wake-up time, reached 15 minutes at a time.
  • Optional reminders to wind down, go to bed, and log your sleep in the morning.
