## Short description (80 characters max)

## Full description (4,000 characters max)

Sleep Tracker shows how much you really sleep and helps you move your bedtime to where it should be, a little at a time.

Log in two taps
Tap "Going to sleep" when you lie down and "I'm awake" when you get up. You can also add or change any night by hand and rate how well you slept.

See your sleep debt
Set a sleep goal that fits your age: the app shows the range sleep experts recommend for you, and you choose a goal within it. See last night at a glance, your sleep debt over the last 14 days, and your sleep by day, week and month. Sleep that crosses midnight counts toward the right days.

A bedtime that fits your wake-up time
Sleep Tracker works out a bedtime from your usual wake-up time, or one you choose, and moves you toward it 15 minutes at a time so the change is easy to keep.

Reminders when you want them
Optional reminders to wind down, go to bed, and log your sleep in the morning. They're scheduled on your phone.

Your data stays yours
Sign in with email or Google and your entries sync across your devices. Export everything as a CSV file whenever you like, and delete your account and all your data from the app at any time. No ads and no analytics.

Sleep Tracker is not a medical device and doesn't give medical advice. If you're worried about your sleep, talk to a doctor.
